## Step 3 - Comparison of Sofort and Mittel

Three of the four runs gave the correct profit of **€930.46**. Both Sofort (Instant) and Mittel (Thinking) were correct when asked to give only the final answer. When asked to show the calculation, Sofort was correct, while Mittel reported €932.46.

Both calculation responses used the correct quantities: 1,035 units initially sold, 72 refunded, 963 paid sales, and 212 unsold units. Mittel made a multiplication error in the revenue after refunds. It reported €55,000.856 for 963 × €57.112, which equals €54,998.856. That error carried through to the final profit, making it €2.00 too high.

Showing the calculation made it possible to pinpoint the error. Sofort was more accurate in these four runs, though this single problem is too small a test to draw a general conclusion about either setting.
